## 🧠 About Me

I am **Abraham Atubra (SCode)** — a Ghanaian software developer, system architect, and educational technologist building mission-critical software ecosystems across **Education, AI infrastructure, and Spatial Computing**.

I design and deploy software ecosystems that:
- 🎓 **Institutionalize Student Credentialing:** Archival verification systems for leadership and academic citations across Africa.
- 📚 **Democratize Educational Access:** AI-driven platforms providing curated repository access, OCR parsing, and past paper analysis.
- 🌍 **Digitize Cultural & Geographic Heritage:** Custom digital registries and spatial mapping integrations for institutional bodies.

> **Mission:** Building resilient, production-grade technology that solves structural challenges across Africa while competing globally.

## 🚀 Key Production Infrastructure

### 🛡️ [The Valtiss](https://thevaltiss.com)
> National student leadership citation archive and digital credential verification registry.

- **Purpose:** Standardized institutional verification platform for Ghanaian tertiary leadership credentials and academic honors.
- **Key Features:** Immutable record verification with prefix encoding (`VT-GH-`), Paystack cross-border billing, automated email forwarding via Cloudflare Email Routing.
- **Core Tech:** Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Node.js, Vercel, Cloudflare, Paystack API.

---

### 🎓 [SCode Academic AI (COE Pasco)](https://scode-coepasco.online/)
> AI-powered educational repository and intelligent tutoring ecosystem for Colleges of Education.

- **Purpose:** Direct access to curated academic archives, past examination breakdowns, and automated document analysis for teacher-trainees.
- **Core Tech:** Next.js, FastAPI, Python PDF parsing pipeline, Supabase, Tailwind CSS.

---

### 🏛️ [Bowiri Traditional Council Digital Heritage Portal](https://github.com/SCodeGit/Bowiri-Traditional-Council)
> Official digital heritage, identity tracking, and cultural preservation system.

- **Purpose:** Centralized census archival system and digital identity management for the Bowiri Traditional Area.
- **Core Tech:** React, Node.js, KoboToolbox dataset integration, cloud data architecture.

---

### 🤖 [SCode AI Models & Local Deployments](https://scodegit.github.io/SCodeAI-Models/)
> Quantized GGUF models fine-tuned for specialized educational and domain-specific tasks.

- **Deployment:** Integrated across local runtime environments via Ollama (`qwen2.5-coder`) and cloud interfaces via Hugging Face.
